# Healthcare Transport Dispatch: Support Complaint Research

Scope: scheduling and dispatch software for NEMT and ambulance/patient transport; researched 2026-10-30. Findings inform synthetic demo support tickets only; nothing here is a customer record or a verbatim review.

## Findings

### Vocabulary dispatchers use
- **A trip is two legs, and the return can float.** A will-call is a return trip with no fixed pickup time: the A leg drops the patient off, and the B leg waits in a "Pending" or "Floating" status on the board until the facility or patient calls. [MediRoutes glossary](https://mediroutes.com/nemt-glossary/will-call)
- **Will-calls come from treatment running long or short.** Dialysis or chemo that ends early or late triggers an unscheduled return, which forces last-minute reassignment and adds to dispatcher load. [iCabbi on will-calls](https://icabbi.com/solved/will-calls-nemt/)
- **Standing order is the broker/facility word for recurring.** A broker facility guide defines it as an appointment recurring at least 3 times a week for 12 weeks (dialysis is the typical case) and routes it to a separate facility line from one-off reservations. [MO HealthNet facility brochure](https://www.modivcare.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/MO-HealthNet-Facility-Brochure.pdf)
- **Level of service sets which vehicle can take the job.** Broker materials list ambulatory, wheelchair, and stretcher service; ambulance tools add BLS, ALS, and CCT, derived from crew certifications and vehicle capability. [MO HealthNet brochure](https://www.modivcare.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/MO-HealthNet-Facility-Brochure.pdf), [AngelTrack CAD](https://angeltrack.com/features/ems-cad-software/)
- **Board and manifest are the daily working surfaces.** The board is the live view of all trips and statuses (expected to reflect "what is happening now"); a manifest is the trip list assigned to a driver or vehicle. Dispatchers also talk about reroutes, failed trips, same-day adds, call-offs, multi-loading, and deadhead (dead) miles. [MediRoutes dispatch](https://mediroutes.com/product/new-dispatch), [Bambi on dispatching challenges](https://www.hibambi.com/blog/challenges-nemt-dispatching-software-solutions)
- **Ambulance-side terms differ.** Units, UHU (unit hour utilization), call taking vs. call booking, round-trip records, and recurring transports for dialysis runs; 911 calls compete with scheduled interfacility work for the same units. [AngelTrack CAD](https://angeltrack.com/features/ems-cad-software/)

### Failure modes
- **Driver app sync and GPS lag in weak coverage.** Delayed or erratic GPS pings, late notifications of new assignments, and trip or route edits that fail to transfer between the driver app and dispatch are the standard connectivity complaints, worse in rural dead zones. [Bambi: driver app problems](https://www.hibambi.com/blog/common-problems-with-nemt-driver-apps-and-solutions)
- **Changes that never reach the driver.** Batch updates, voice-only changes, and unacknowledged cancellations leave drivers working from stale trip details, producing wasted trips and missed pickups. [NEMT Platform on communication](https://nemtplatform.com/blogs/how-nemt-software-can-improve-communication-between-dispatchers-and-drivers), [Bambi on communication](https://www.hibambi.com/blog/improve-communication-dispatchers-drivers-nemt)
- **Reassignment is slow when a driver calls off.** Without automation, a cancellation sends dispatchers into phone calls and manifest rework that can take an hour; vendors pitch one-click reassignment and live broadcast of a failed trip to the nearest driver. [Bambi](https://www.hibambi.com/blog/challenges-nemt-dispatching-software-solutions), [MediRoutes](https://mediroutes.com/product/new-dispatch)
- **Broker trip uploads are re-keyed by hand.** Staff download CSV or spreadsheet files from separate broker portals and retype them, producing wrong addresses, dropped special instructions, duplicates, and orphaned legs (a pickup with no linked return). [NEMT Platform on manual uploads](https://nemtplatform.com/blogs/manual-uploads-trip-errors-and-delays-the-hidden-cost-to-your-nemt-operation)
- **Dispatch data does not flow into billing or the trip log.** Trips without GPS proof, authorization confirmation, or accurate timestamps leave audit gaps and denied claims; ambulance shops cite missing times, locations, or service level as claim rejections. [Mindbowser](https://www.mindbowser.com/nemt-dispatch-problems/), [AngelTrack CAD](https://angeltrack.com/features/ems-cad-software/)
- **Scheduling UI complaints are about density, the auto-scheduler, and billing.** Small-agency reviewers of TripMaster mention a scheduling screen hard to read on small displays, an auto-scheduler that sometimes ignores required parameters, billing problems with a specific broker, and passwords that expire too often. [TripMaster reviews on SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/software/product/Trip-Master)
- **Ambulance CAD criticism is thin and dated.** A third-party Traumasoft profile (3 reviews) notes a counter-intuitive CAD grid layout and dependence on a constant data connection. [IntuitionLabs Traumasoft profile](https://intuitionlabs.ai/software/emergency-medical-services-ems/computer-aided-dispatch-cad-integration/traumasoft)

### Severity and why it matters
- **Late dialysis pickups cost treatment time and chairs.** Each ride must hit the center's intake window; a late return holds a chair for the next patient, and missed sessions carry clinical risk and can push a facility below quality thresholds. [RouteGenie dialysis guide](https://routegenie.com/blog/dialysis-transportation-nemt-providers/)
- **Broker contracts put numbers and fines on lateness.** Examples: on time means up to 15 minutes after the scheduled pickup (Kansas) or plus/minus 15 (New York); will-calls due back within about 1 hour and hospital discharges within 3 hours; Mississippi penalties up to $5,000 per instance. The same guide cites an audit where 78% of complaints were about late trips and 14% about no-shows. [Bambi KPI guide](https://www.hibambi.com/blog/ultimate-guide-to-nemt-kpis)
- **Discharges are a named urgent class.** Broker guidance says hospital discharge reservations must come from facility staff, same-day discharges get picked up within 3 hours, and a delayed or cancelled discharge must be reported immediately. [MO HealthNet brochure](https://www.modivcare.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/MO-HealthNet-Facility-Brochure.pdf)
